== TEXT ==

Promoting a scientific culture among youths in Congo DRC.

[[FIGURE-ROW]]
Image: sst-gombe-village.jpg
Caption: Visiting schools attend the Village of Sciences at the Institute de la Gombe in Kinshasa.
[[/FIGURE-ROW]]

The [Semaine de la Science et des Technologies (SST)](https://www.semainedelasciencerdc.org) is a festival organized in the Democratic Republic of Congo. This event has three objectives:

1. To develop a scientific and technological culture in Africa
1. To promote African knowledge and know-how in these fields
1. Above all: to encourage vocations

> **The festival's motto: Science is fun, join us! :-)**

The figure behind this event: Professor Raïssa Malu. Physicist and author born in Kinshasa, she is the founder and director of the non-profit association Investing In People, one of the SST organizers.

I met Raïssa Malu at the African School of Physics of December 2022 (ASP2022) and she invited me for the special 10<sup>th</sup> edition in April 2023. I had the honour to be a keynote speaker and showcase the ATLAS and DUNE experiments at universities in Kinshasa and Mbanza-Ngungu. The SST scientific director Dora Muanda trained volunteering local students to prepare and animate booths at the Village of Sciences. A total of 500 nearby schools visited the Institut de la Gombe over two days. I had the privilege to showcase CERN's Large Hadron Collider and the ATLAS Experiment to more than 10,000 students!

The festival is much more than the Village of Sciences: there were conferences, panels, opening and closing ceremonies: have a look at the [summary of SST 10<sup>th</sup> edition 2023](https://www.semainedelasciencerdc.org/programme-edition-2023/).

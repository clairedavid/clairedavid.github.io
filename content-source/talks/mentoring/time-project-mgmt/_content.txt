Time & Project Management

== TEXT ==

Behind every achievement lies effective organisation. In this interactive talk, I will give you tips and tricks to better manage your time, your projects, and your mental health. I will introduce the pacing technique called Pomodoro, as well as the Eisenhower Matrix for prioritizing your tasks, SMART goals and their pros and cons, and the Gantt chart to visualize a schedule. I will then showcase free online tools, some of which I use every day, to better organize your work. Come with a pen, some paper and your good mood!

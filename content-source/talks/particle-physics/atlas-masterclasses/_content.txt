ATLAS Masterclasses

== TEXT ==

What do experimental particle physicists do? What do experimental particle physicists need? This talk explains the science done at the Large Hadron Collider and serves as introduction to the Masterclass hands-on session, where high school students have the opportunity to perform a simplified data analysis during an afternoon.

This talk was presented during the ATLAS Masterclass 2014 at the University of Victoria. It was organized by Vispa.
